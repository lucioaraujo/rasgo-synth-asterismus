Tutorial

The same guide that opens from the app's GUIDE button, in nine parts: from the seed to the recording.

1. START · SEED AND TERRITORY

A seed opens a musical possibility: modules, roles, material, tempo and a narrative. Type a number or use ALEA. It also defines initial module values; changing it must not erase the drawing already made in the Field.

2. COMPOSE IN THE FIELD

Drag module circles to establish positions, neighbourhoods and connections. The performer’s drawing is spatial memory and changes only by intentional action.

3. READ THE CONSTELLATION

The Constellation is orientation, not a preset browser. Distance, connection, movement and depth must produce musical change.

4. AXIS OF FORCES

The orange body controls AUTONOMY, INTERVENTION, MEMORY and RISK without inverse pairs: both may be zero. The blue body controls DENSITY, TENSION, SPACE and DEPTH. The two bodies have four independent macros each. Every movement must be audible. The round knobs beside it are the same eight values: the axis is the gesture, the knobs the fine adjustment.

5. SCENES AND GESTURES

NASCENTE, ESPIRAL, RASGO and BALANÇO transform the material without interrupting a performance already in PLAY. CAPTURAR stores a scene; DESFAZER returns to the previous capture. HOLD, OPEN, TEAR, REPAIR, STUTTER and DISPLACE (shifts the stem's pulse and phase; the tempo stays) act on existing material.

6. LISTEN AND ORCHESTRATE

Each module is a star with its own stem: up to eight bodies in the Field, each on its own channel in the Matter Field. A star at zero presence is unlit and uses no processing; raising its presence lights it. Lit stars relate through the sky’s web — answers, contagion, shadows and hockets — without one star’s sound leaking into another’s channel. Each material serves a role — pulse, trajectory, line, field, rupture, call, memory or trace. Use presence, mix, mute, solo and local controls to reveal or withdraw those roles. MASTER sets final level only.

7. THE SKY · WEB, FORM, TIME AND MOTION

TRAMA switches on the sky's web: every bar the sky chooses pairs in focus (answer, contagion, shadow, hocket, pedal, friction, cadence) and the piece travels a form — OPENING, DEVELOPMENT, CONTRAST, RETURN — shown in the header. Time breathes (TEMPO 1.04×), swing comes from an orbit, each star counts its own cycle and the bar groups as 3+3+2 and the like. In development one star DRIFTS against the pulse; computed moons bring ECLIPSES. In the Constellation the stars move by physics — gravity, springs, pendulums, billiards, magnetism, spin, foam — and leave trails; strong collisions sound. Each star sounds in the stereo field where it is in the Constellation (the bass stays centred); ESPAÇO opens that field. VARIAÇÃO lets the sky light and dim stars in the mixer: the fader moves by itself (orange thumb) and returns to your value when you grab it. With TRAMA off the instrument sounds exactly as before.

8. THE LIBRARY · SWAP AND PIN STARS

The seed chooses the stars among all 28 of the sky (at most one CONTROL star per sky); the LIBRARY groups them by family. Click a star in the Constellation, then the LIBRARY button (▾; with no star in focus, it asks which body): the menu shows the body's star, PIN THIS STAR and the families — TONE, TEXTURE, VOICE, PULSE and CONTROL. Choose another star and the body plays it; BACK TO THE SEED'S STAR undoes it. Or drag from the LIBRARY strip and drop onto a star: the same menu opens for it, even with all 8 places full. A pinned star (amber diamond) is not dimmed by VARIATION or by eclipses. CONTROL stars make no sound: lit, they filter the others' attacks — SIEVE (Xenakis's sieve), POLYMETER (each star in its own cycle), HOCKET (one attack at a time) and RUPTURE (the sky falls silent). The MODAL lives in one body only.

9. RECORD A REALISATION

PLAY only plays. REC starts recording a 24-bit stereo WAV take (and starts playing if stopped); while recording, the transport box and the window frame turn red with the take time, and the button becomes CODA: it requests the coda and closes the file after its tail — amber, CODA · FINISHING. The file path appears in the status line. TRACK · DURATION (1, 2, 3 or 5 MIN): one click records at once, with a countdown; the sky's form fits the duration — opening, development, contrast, return — with the PATH switching scenes, and the coda arrives at the end. While recording, the piece also ends by itself when you let the instrument run untouched for a while (about 100 to 190 beats) after the seed has gone through a few turns; the header warns CODA SOON. After the coda only the recording closes: the sound goes on and a new piece opens (in an ALBUM, the next track comes in by itself). With the sound already playing, the take opens with a 1.5 s fade-in. STOP is a technical interruption, not a coda.

CENTRAL RULE

Every visible form and control must have a clear musical consequence.
